# Introducing OpenAI Presence

## Annotation

Official product announcement for OpenAI's managed enterprise agent-deployment offering.

## Relevant evidence

Presence combines policies, guardrails, approved actions, simulations, evaluation tooling, escalation rules, and a Codex-assisted improvement loop. It is available to eligible enterprise customers through limited general availability rather than as a self-serve product.
