# Parameter-Efficient Retrievers for Polish and European Languages

## Annotation

Preprint on compact multilingual dense retrievers trained through cross-lingual alignment, relational knowledge distillation, and contrastive fine-tuning using teacher-generated supervision.

## Relevant evidence

The authors evaluate PolDense and EuroDense across 41 Polish and 150 multilingual retrieval tasks. They report that PolDense-1B outperforms evaluated retrievers up to 9B parameters and that EuroDense-435M ranks first among evaluated sub-1B models by task- and language-averaged performance. These are author-reported preprint results and should not be generalized beyond the evaluated datasets without independent replication.
