# Predicting Partial Answer Quality and Utility in Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation

## Annotation

Original preprint studying intermediate answer quality and marginal utility across iterative retrieval/reasoning trajectories.

## Relevant evidence

The authors probe intermediate answers after retrieval/reasoning iterations and report frequent quality plateaus. Their prediction-based early-stopping experiments reduce average iteration count by about 11% while retaining about 98% of natural-stopping final answer quality. Results are author-reported preprint evidence and may not transfer to other corpora, models, or production workloads.
