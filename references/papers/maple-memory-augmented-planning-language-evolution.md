# MAPLE: Memory-Augmented Planning with Language and Evolution

## Annotation

Original arXiv preprint by Kesheng Chen, Yamin Hu, and Wenjian Luo. The paper studies maintaining an optimization problem through successive natural-language revisions while preserving executable state and prior search information.

## Relevant evidence

The authors describe retaining the optimization program, accepted plans, earlier updates, and candidate solutions. They introduce NLDO with 15 trajectories and 180 updates and report completion of all trajectories, online scalar quality of 0.951, and a Pareto hypervolume ratio of 0.875. Controlled comparisons report benefits from maintaining executable state across revisions.

This is a mutable arXiv preprint and the reported results have not been treated here as independently replicated.
