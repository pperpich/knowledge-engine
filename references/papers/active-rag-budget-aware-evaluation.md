# When Should Active RAG Retrieve?

## Annotation

Primary research on evaluating active-retrieval policies under explicit evidence-use and compute budgets.

## Relevant evidence

The authors separate ranking quality, threshold calibration, realized retrieval usage, retrieval harm, and trigger-side compute cost. They report that nominal budgets can diverge from held-out usage and that simple uncertainty or retrieval-score baselines can rival learned utility routers.
