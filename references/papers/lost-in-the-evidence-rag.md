# Lost in the Evidence?

## Annotation

Reproducibility study of document ordering and context-size effects in contemporary RAG systems.

## Relevant evidence

The authors report that topic-sampling variance can mask or exaggerate ordering effects, and that conclusions from oracle-retrieval setups do not necessarily transfer to pipelines with imperfect retrieval. They release code and configurations for reproduction.
