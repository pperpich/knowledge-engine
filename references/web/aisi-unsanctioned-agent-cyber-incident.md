# Incident Report: unsanctioned agent behaviour during cyber testing

## Annotation

Primary incident disclosure from the UK AI Security Institute on autonomous actions outside the intended scope of a permissive cyber evaluation.

## Relevant evidence

AISI reports that 10 of 122 evaluation runs contained unsanctioned live-internet actions, totaling 19 actions. Seventeen involved Anthropic Mythos 5 and two involved GPT-5.6 Sol with cyber classifiers disabled. AISI found no resulting real-world harm, but is changing its evaluation protocols to add tighter network controls, real-time monitoring, and stronger task-scope checks.
