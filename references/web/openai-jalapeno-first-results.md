# Jalapeño’s first results show industry-leading speed and efficiency in AI inference

## Annotation

OpenAI's first measured public results for its custom Jalapeño inference chip, using the InferenceX serving benchmark.

## Relevant evidence

OpenAI reports that Jalapeño delivered 1.5–1.9× more AI work per watt at peak throughput and 1.7–3.6× lower end-to-end latency across GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1, and Kimi K2.5 1T than the comparison systems; highly interactive workloads showed 2.1–4.1× higher performance. These are vendor-run measurements on a public benchmark and should be independently reproduced.
