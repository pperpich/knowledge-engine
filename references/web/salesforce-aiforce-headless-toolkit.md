# Salesforce Unveils AIforce, Bringing the Full Power of Its Platform to Any Interface

## Annotation

First-party launch description for AIforce and its Headless Toolkit, which expose Salesforce context, permissions, business logic, and actions to external AI interfaces.

## Relevant evidence

Salesforce says the Headless Toolkit exposes platform capabilities through MCPs, APIs, plug-ins, skills, and developer tools. It says requests use existing Salesforce permissions and business rules, and that Salesforce in Claude is available to customers in beta. Availability and performance claims are vendor-reported.
