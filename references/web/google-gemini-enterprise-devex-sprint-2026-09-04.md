# Driving Developer Excellence: Inside the Program Sprints

## Annotation

Google Cloud’s September 4 account of its Gemini Enterprise developer-experience governance sprint and the operational/documentation changes made while testing a governed-agent path end to end.

## Relevant evidence

Google describes a workflow from dedicated agent identity through registry, default-deny gateway binding, access/content-safety policy, and verification with audit trails. The post says the sprint hardened prerequisites, fail-closed Model Armor examples, policy-evaluation guidance, private networking setup, CEL syntax, and log-query guidance. This is evidence of developer-platform/documentation hardening, not a new governance product launch.
